1从文字到三元组From text to triples

知识图谱的基本单位是三元组：（主语，关系，宾语）。这里用句式规则抽取（实际中多由大模型完成）；代词 She / He / It 指向文档开头的主语。人物档案常把公司写成简称（Gasel），公司文档却写全称（Gasel Bank Ltd.），所以还要做实体消解，把同一实体的不同叫法合成一个节点。 The basic unit of a knowledge graph is a triple: (subject, relation, object). Here sentence patterns do the extraction (in practice usually an LLM); the pronouns She / He / It refer to the document's first subject. People's profiles often shorten a company's name (Gasel) while company documents use the full one (Gasel Bank Ltd.), so entity resolution must merge different names of the same entity into one node.

4全局问题与 GraphRAGGlobal questions and GraphRAG

“哪家公司员工最多？”这类问题要看完整个语料：检索前 k 篇只能数到 k 个。图数据库可以直接计数。GraphRAG（微软，2024）的做法是先在图上找出社区（连接紧密的群体），为每个社区预先写好摘要；全局问题来了，就对所有社区摘要做 map-reduce。 Questions like “which company employs the most people?” need the whole corpus: retrieving the top k can count at most k. A graph database counts directly. GraphRAG (Microsoft, 2024) finds communities (densely linked groups) in the graph and writes a summary for each in advance; a global question is then answered by map-reduce over all the community summaries.
