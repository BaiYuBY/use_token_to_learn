Temperature T divides the logits by T: small T sharpens, large T flattens. top-k keeps the k most likely words; top-p keeps the fewest words whose probabilities add up to p; min-p keeps words at least min-p times as likely as the top one. 4束搜索：同时保留几条候选Beam search: keep several candidates at once

贪心解码每步只看眼前最好的词，可能错过整体概率更高的句子。束搜索每步保留得分最高的 B 条候选（得分 = 累计的对数概率）。拖动束宽，看找到的句子和它的对数概率。 Greedy decoding only looks at the best word right now and can miss a sentence that is more likely overall. Beam search keeps the B best candidates at every step (score = summed log-probability). Drag the beam width and see the sentence found and its log-probability.

5长文本的重复与惩罚Repetition in long text, and penalties

让模型连续写 60 个词。贪心解码很容易陷入循环，一遍遍写同一句话。橙色标出的是前面出现过的四个词的组合。试试加大重复惩罚（把已出现词的 logit 除以 r）、频率惩罚（减去 出现次数 × f）或存在惩罚（出现过就减去 p），或者改用采样。 Let the model write 60 words in a row. Greedy decoding easily falls into a loop, writing the same sentence over and over. Orange marks 4-word sequences that already appeared. Try a repetition penalty (divide the logits of seen words by r), a frequency penalty (subtract count × f), a presence penalty (subtract p once a word has appeared), or switch to sampling.

6约束解码：保证输出合乎格式Constrained decoding: guarantee the format

把格式规则（这里是小英语的语法；实际中常是 JSON Schema 或正则表达式）编译成一个状态机。每一步只允许状态机接受的词，把其他词的 logit 设为 −∞，再照常采样。打开约束、把温度调得很高，句子依然全部合乎语法。 Compile the format rules (here the tiny English grammar; in practice often a JSON Schema or a regular expression) into a state machine. At each step only words the machine accepts are allowed: the other logits are set to −∞, then sampling proceeds as usual. With the constraint on, even a very high temperature produces only grammatical sentences.
