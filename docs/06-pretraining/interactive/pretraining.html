预训练 · 交互演示Pre-training · Interactive Playground

预训练只有一个目标：预测下一个词。本页的模型、曲线和预测结果都来自本章代码的真实训练。 Pre-training has a single goal: predict the next token. The models, curves and predictions on this page all come from real training runs in this chapter's code.

1预测下一个词Predicting the next word

两个模型结构相同，只是训练数据不同：一个只见过 100 个句子，另一个见过 20,000 个。点一个词，看模型在它之前的上下文下预测的概率。词的颜色表示模型给正确答案的概率（绿＝高，红＝低）。 Both models have the same architecture but different training data: one saw only 100 sentences, the other 20,000. Click a word to see what the model predicted from the context before it. Word colours show the probability given to the correct word (green = high, red = low).

2交叉熵与困惑度Cross-entropy and perplexity

每个位置的损失是 −ln p(正确的词)。把所有位置平均就是交叉熵；困惑度 = e交叉熵，可以理解为“模型平均在几个词之间犹豫”。拖动滑块改变模型给正确答案的概率。 The loss at each position is −ln p(correct word). Averaged over positions it is the cross-entropy; perplexity = ecross-entropy, roughly “how many words the model is hesitating between”. Drag the slider to change the probability given to the correct word.

4数据配比与重复轮数Data mixture and repetition

高质量数据（书籍、维基百科、数学）往往很少，配比给高了就要重复很多轮（epoch）。研究发现，重复大约 4 轮以内几乎和新数据一样有用，之后收益迅速下降。调整训练总量和各来源的权重，观察每个来源要被重复几轮。 High-quality data (books, Wikipedia, maths) is scarce, so giving it a large weight means repeating it for many epochs. Research found that up to about 4 epochs of repetition is almost as useful as fresh data, with quickly diminishing returns after that. Change the token budget and the weights and see how many epochs each source needs.

6背诵还是泛化？Memorizing or generalizing?

训练损失低不代表学会了。要看模型没见过的验证数据，以及它是否掌握了规则：在 “the cat that chases the dogs ___” 中，最近的名词是复数，但动词必须跟主语 cat 一致用单数。 A low training loss does not mean the model has learned. Look at unseen validation data, and at whether the model has learned the rules: in “the cat that chases the dogs ___” the nearest noun is plural, but the verb must agree with the subject “cat” and be singular.

7训练要花多少算力？How much compute does training take?

训练一个参数量为 N 的模型、看 D 个词元，大约需要 6·N·D 次浮点运算（前向 2ND，反向约 4ND）。MFU 是实际用到的算力占硬件峰值的比例，大规模训练通常在 30%–50%。 Training a model with N parameters on D tokens takes about 6·N·D floating-point operations (forward 2ND, backward about 4ND). MFU is the fraction of the hardware's peak actually achieved, typically 30%–50% at scale.
