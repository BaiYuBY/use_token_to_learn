2叠加与稀疏自编码器Superposition and sparse autoencoders

特征往往比维度多。一个玩具模型要把 5 个特征压进 2 维再还原：特征经常同时出现时，它只存 2 个；特征很少同时出现时，它把 5 个都存进去，排成五边形，代价是偶尔互相干扰。这就是叠加，也是单个神经元难以解释的原因。拖动滑块： There are usually more features than dimensions. A toy model must squeeze 5 features into 2 dimensions and back: when features often co-occur, it stores only about 2; when they rarely co-occur, it stores all 5 as a pentagon, at the cost of occasional interference. 3幻觉：为什么模型会编造Hallucination: why models make things up

一个小模型学习 5000 条“某人在某公司工作”，每条在训练数据里出现 0、1、2、4 或 8 次。问它没见过的人，它也照样说出一家公司：它只会续写，没有“不知道”这个选项。是猜还是弃答更划算，取决于怎么评分。拖动答错的扣分： A small model learns 5,000 “X works at Y” facts, each appearing 0, 1, 2, 4 or 8 times in training. Ask about someone it never saw and it still names a company: it can only continue text, with no “I don't know” option. Whether guessing or abstaining pays depends on the grading.
