2残差连接：让梯度传得回去Residual connections: letting gradients get back

一叠随机初始化的层（每层 h → tanh(W·h)）。柱子是反向传播时每一层收到的梯度大小（相对于最顶层，对数刻度）。不加残差时，梯度每往下一层就被乘一次，很快消失；加上残差 h → h + tanh(W·h)，每一层都有一条“直通”的路。 A stack of randomly initialised layers (each h → tanh(W·h)). Bars show the gradient each layer receives during backprop (relative to the top layer, log scale). Without residuals the gradient is multiplied once per layer and quickly vanishes; with residuals, h → h + tanh(W·h), every layer has a direct path.

3LayerNorm 与 RMSNormLayerNorm vs RMSNorm

归一化把每个词元的向量拉回到稳定的数值范围。LayerNorm 先减去均值再除以标准差；RMSNorm 省掉了减均值，只除以均方根（RMS），更简单也更快，Llama 等模型都用它。试试几个按钮。 Normalization pulls each token's vector back into a stable range. LayerNorm subtracts the mean and divides by the standard deviation; RMSNorm skips the mean and only divides by the root-mean-square, which is simpler and faster and is used by Llama and others. Try the buttons.

4前馈网络与 SwiGLUThe feed-forward network and SwiGLU

注意力负责在词元之间“交流”，前馈网络（FFN）则对每个词元单独加工：先把 d 维升到更宽的 d_ff，经过非线性，再压回 d 维。SwiGLU 用两条支路：一条经过 SiLU 作为“门”，另一条作为“值”，两者相乘。拖动滑块看门怎样控制信号。 Attention lets tokens “talk to each other”; the feed-forward network (FFN) processes each token on its own: widen from d to d_ff, apply a non-linearity, project back to d. SwiGLU has two branches: one goes through SiLU as a “gate”, the other is the “value”, and they are multiplied. Drag the sliders to see the gate at work.
