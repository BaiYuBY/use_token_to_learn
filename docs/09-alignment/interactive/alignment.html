SFT 之后，怎样让模型的回答更符合人的偏好？本页的实验来自本章代码在 CPU 上的真实训练：一个隐藏的“人类”给句子打分，奖励模型只能从两两比较中去猜它的喜好，再用 RLHF 或 DPO 调整模型。 After SFT, how do we make a model's answers match what people prefer? The experiments on this page are real CPU training runs of this chapter's code: a hidden “human” scores sentences, a reward model can only guess its taste from pairwise choices, and RLHF or DPO then adjusts the model.

1偏好数据：人只需说“哪个更好”Preference data: people only say which is better

给人打绝对分数很难，比较两个回答容易得多。Bradley–Terry 模型假设：A 比 B 好的概率 = sigmoid(r(A) − r(B))。奖励差越大，选择越确定；奖励相同时就是抛硬币，所以偏好数据天然带噪声。拖动两个回答的奖励看看。 Giving absolute scores is hard; comparing two answers is much easier. The Bradley–Terry model assumes P(A better than B) = sigmoid(r(A) − r(B)). The larger the reward gap, the surer the choice; with equal rewards it is a coin flip, so preference data is noisy by nature. Drag the two rewards.

4三种方法对比：best-of-n、RLHF、DPOThree methods compared: best-of-n, RLHF, DPO

横轴是与 SFT 模型的 KL 距离（改动有多大），纵轴是真实奖励。best-of-n 最简单：采样 n 个，留奖励模型最喜欢的那个，KL 恰好等于 log n − (n−1)/n。点击图例可以显示或隐藏。 x is the KL distance from the SFT model (how much it changed), y is the true reward. Best-of-n is the simplest: sample n, keep the one the reward model likes most; its KL is exactly log n − (n−1)/n. Click the legend to show or hide a series.

5DPO：跳过奖励模型，直接从偏好对学习DPO: skip the reward model and learn from the pairs

DPO 把第 2 节的闭式解反过来用：奖励可以写成 β·log(π/πref)。于是损失只需要两个回答在当前模型和 SFT 模型下的对数概率。拖动两个“对数概率变化”，看损失和梯度权重：模型已经分对的样本权重小，分错的样本权重大。 DPO runs the closed form of part 2 backwards: the reward can be written as β·log(π/πref). The loss then needs only the log-probabilities of the two answers under the current model and the SFT model. Drag the two log-probability changes to see the loss and the gradient weight: pairs the model already gets right weigh little, wrong ones weigh a lot.

6Constitutional AI：让 AI 按原则给反馈Constitutional AI: AI feedback guided by principles

人工标注又贵又慢。Constitutional AI 写下一组原则（“宪法”），让模型自己批评、修改回答，再让 AI 按原则在两个回答中选一个，代替人工标注偏好（RLAIF）。下面是一个手写的示意：勾选不同的原则，看 AI 评审会选哪个回答。 Human labels are slow and expensive. Constitutional AI writes down a set of principles (a “constitution”), has the model critique and revise its own answers, then has an AI pick between two answers according to the principles, replacing human preference labels (RLAIF).
