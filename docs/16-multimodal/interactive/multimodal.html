1图像切成小块，每块是一个词元Cut the image into patches, one token per patch

ViT 把图像切成 p×p 的小块，把每块的像素（p×p×3 个数）展平成一个向量，再乘一个矩阵变成词元向量。这一步就是步长为 p 的卷积。点图上的一块，看它展平后的样子。本章的 ViT 用 p = 4：16×16 的图变成 16 个词元，再加一个 [CLS] 词元。 A ViT cuts the image into p×p patches, flattens each patch's pixels (p×p×3 numbers) into a vector and multiplies by a matrix to get a token vector. That step is a convolution with stride p. Click a patch to see it flattened. This chapter's ViT uses p = 4: a 16×16 image becomes 16 tokens, plus one [CLS] token.

2CLIP：一批图和一批描述的相似度矩阵CLIP: the similarity matrix of a batch of pictures and captions

图像编码器和文本编码器各把输入变成一个单位向量，两者的点积就是余弦相似度。训练目标：在一批 N 对里，第 i 张图要在 N 条描述中挑中第 i 条（按行做 softmax 交叉熵），第 i 条描述也要挑中第 i 张图（按列）。乘在相似度前面的“温度倒数” s 决定 softmax 有多尖：本章模型学到的是 。拖动它，看损失怎么变。 The image encoder and the text encoder each turn their input into a unit vector; their dot product is the cosine similarity. The objective: in a batch of N pairs, image i must pick caption i among the N captions (softmax cross-entropy along each row), and caption i must pick image i (along each column). The inverse temperature s multiplying the similarities sets how sharp the softmax is: this chapter's model learned . Drag it and watch the loss.

3零样本分类：用文字当分类器Zero-shot classification: text as the classifier

不训练任何分类头：把 16 个颜色-形状组合各写成一句提示，比如 “a small red circle on the left”，算出它们的文本向量；图像向量和哪个提示最近，就归为哪类。“提示词集成”把 4 个模板（大小 × 左右）的向量取平均，减少某一种措辞的偏差。点一张图。 No classifier head is trained: write each of the 16 colour-shape pairs as a prompt such as “a small red circle on the left”, embed the prompts, and assign the image to the closest one. “Prompt ensembling” averages the vectors of 4 templates (size × side) to reduce the bias of any one wording. 4视觉语言模型：看图写描述A vision-language model writes captions
