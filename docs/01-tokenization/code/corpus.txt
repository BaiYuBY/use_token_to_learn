A language model reads text as a sequence of tokens. A token can be a word, a piece of a word, a single character, or even a single byte.
The model never sees letters directly. It sees token ids, which are integers, and it looks up one vector for each id.
Tokenization is the first step of every language model, and the last step too: the model predicts the next token, and the tokenizer turns the tokens back into text.
Byte pair encoding starts from bytes and repeatedly merges the most frequent pair of neighbouring tokens into a new token.
After many merges, frequent words such as the, model and token become single tokens, while rare words are split into smaller pieces.
The tokenizer learns its merges once, before training the model, and then keeps them fixed.
A larger vocabulary makes sequences shorter, but the embedding table and the output layer become larger.
Numbers like 2024, 3.14159 and 1000000 are often split into several tokens, which is one reason why models find arithmetic hard.
Spaces matter: " hello" and "hello" are different tokens, and so are "Hello" and "hello".

语言模型把文本看作一串词元。词元可以是一个词、一个词的一部分、一个字，甚至是一个字节。
模型从来不会直接看到文字，它看到的是词元编号，也就是整数，然后为每个编号查出一个向量。
分词是每个语言模型的第一步，也是最后一步：模型预测下一个词元，分词器再把词元还原成文本。
字节对编码从字节开始，不断把最常见的相邻词元对合并成一个新的词元。
经过多次合并，常见的词会变成一个词元，罕见的词会被拆成更小的片段。
一个汉字在 UTF-8 中通常占三个字节，所以没有学过中文的分词器会把一个汉字拆成多个词元。
词表越大，序列越短，但是嵌入矩阵和输出层也越大。
