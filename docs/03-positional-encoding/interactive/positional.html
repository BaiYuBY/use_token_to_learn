2正弦位置编码：一组转速不同的时钟Sinusoidal encoding: a set of clocks at different speeds

每两维是一对 (sin, cos)，可以看成一个时钟的指针：位置每加 1，第 i 个时钟转过 ωi = 1 / base2i/d 弧度。前面的时钟转得快（像秒针），后面的转得慢（像时针），合起来就能唯一地表示每个位置。在热力图上点一行，或拖动滑块选位置。 Every two dimensions form a (sin, cos) pair, like the hand of a clock: each step in position turns clock i by ωi = 1 / base2i/d radians. The first clocks spin fast (like a second hand), the last ones slowly (like an hour hand), and together they identify every position. Click a row of the heatmap or drag the slider to pick a position.

点积只取决于相对距离The dot product depends only on the distance

曲线是 PE[p] · PE[p + k]，横轴是相对距离 k。拖动上面的“位置”滑块改变 p，曲线完全不动：两个位置的相似度只和它们相隔多远有关。 The curve is PE[p] · PE[p + k] against the offset k. Drag the position slider above to change p: the curve does not move at all, because the similarity of two positions depends only on how far apart they are.

3RoPE：把 q 和 k 按位置旋转RoPE: rotate q and k by their positions

RoPE 不把位置向量“加”到输入上，而是在计算注意力分数前，把 query 和 key 的每一对分量按位置旋转。下面画的是其中一对：q 在位置 m 转过 m·θ，k 在位置 n 转过 n·θ，它们的夹角只差 (m − n)·θ。点“同时右移”，两个位置一起变，分数却保持不变。 RoPE does not add a position vector to the input. Before scores are computed, it rotates every pair of components of the query and the key by their positions. One pair is drawn below: q at position m turns by m·θ and k at position n by n·θ, so their angle changes by (m − n)·θ. Press “shift both” and both positions move while the score stays the same.

4RoPE 的 base：远处的词能“看”多清楚RoPE's base: how clearly distant words are seen

假设 q 和 k 完全相同，曲线显示它们相隔不同距离时的注意力分数（已归一化）。距离越远，各对分量转过的角度越乱，分数整体下降。调大 base，所有时钟都转得更慢，远距离的衰减就变缓。长上下文模型常这样做，例如 Llama 3 用了 500,000。 Assume q and k are identical; the curve shows their (normalized) score at different distances. The farther apart they are, the more scrambled the rotation angles and the lower the score. Increasing the base slows every clock down so distant scores decay more gently. Long-context models do this; Llama 3, for example, uses 500,000.
