混合专家 MoE · 交互演示Mixture of Experts · Playground

MoE 把一个大的前馈层换成很多个小“专家”，每个词元只交给其中几个。参数多（存得多），计算少（每个词元只算几个专家）。难点在于让路由器把词元分得均匀。第 1 节的路由来自一个在 06 章语言上训练的小 MoE 模型。 MoE replaces one big feed-forward layer with many small “experts” and sends each token to only a few. Many parameters (lots stored), little compute (each token runs only a few experts). The hard part is making the router spread tokens evenly. Section 1's routes come from a small MoE model trained on chapter 06's language.

1路由：每个词元交给哪两个专家Routing: which two experts get each token

每层 8 个专家，每个词元选 2 个（top-2）。按钮颜色是第一选择，下面的小字是两个专家及其门控权重。点一个词元，看路由器给 8 个专家的概率。右边是专家分工：每类词有多大比例把第一选择交给某个专家。没有人告诉模型什么是“动词”，分工是训练中自己形成的。 8 experts per layer, 2 chosen per token (top-2). A button's colour is the first choice; the small text gives both experts and their gate weights. Click a token to see the router's probabilities over the 8 experts. On the right is the division of labour: for each word type, the share of tokens whose first choice is each expert. Nobody told the model what a “verb” is; the specialization emerged in training.

2负载均衡模拟器Load-balancing simulator

路由器天生偏心：一开始稍微好一点的专家拿到更多词元、学得更快、又拿到更多……专家分布在不同 GPU 上时，最忙的那张卡决定每一步的耗时；设了容量上限时，超出的词元会被丢掉（只走残差连接）。辅助损失（Switch Transformer）在训练目标里惩罚不均衡；偏置调整（DeepSeek-V3）只在“选专家”时给每个专家加一个偏置，负载高就调低，不改变训练目标。下面的模拟里，每一步有 512 个词元，路由器对专家有固定的偏好（偏好越不均，倾斜越大）。 Routers are naturally biased: an expert that starts slightly better gets more tokens, learns faster and gets even more. With experts on different GPUs, the busiest GPU sets the pace of every step; with a capacity limit, overflow tokens are dropped (they only pass through the residual). The auxiliary loss (Switch Transformer) penalizes imbalance in the training objective; bias adjustment (DeepSeek-V3) adds a per-expert bias only when choosing experts and lowers it for busy ones, leaving the objective untouched. In the simulation, each step routes 512 tokens and the router has fixed preferences (more skew, more tilt).

4总参数、激活参数与专家并行Total parameters, active parameters and expert parallelism

总参数 = 其余部分（注意力、嵌入、稠密层）+ MoE 层数 ×（路由专家 + 共享专家）× 每个专家的参数；激活参数只算被选中的 k 个专家和共享专家。显存要装下总参数，计算只按激活参数。专家并行（EP）把专家分到多张卡上，每个词元在每个 MoE 层都要被发给它选中的专家所在的卡，算完再收回来（两次 all-to-all）。 Total = everything else (attention, embeddings, dense layers) + MoE layers × (routed + shared experts) × parameters per expert; active counts only the k chosen experts and the shared ones. Memory must hold the total; compute follows the active count. Expert parallelism (EP) spreads experts over GPUs, so in every MoE layer each token is sent to the GPUs of its chosen experts and the results come back (two all-to-alls).
