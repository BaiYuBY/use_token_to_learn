1LoRA：冻结 W，只学一个低秩的修改量LoRA: freeze W and learn only a low-rank change

全参数微调要更新整个 d×d 的权重矩阵 W。LoRA 把 W 冻结，另外学两个小矩阵：B（d×r）和 A（r×d），用 W + (α/r)·BA 代替 W。r 远小于 d，所以要训练的参数少得多。训练完可以把 BA 直接加进 W，推理时没有任何额外开销。 Full fine-tuning updates the whole d×d weight matrix W. LoRA freezes W and learns two small matrices instead, B (d×r) and A (r×d), using W + (α/r)·BA in place of W. Since r is much smaller than d, far fewer parameters are trained. Afterwards BA can be added into W, so inference costs nothing extra.

5显存账：为什么 LoRA 和 QLoRA 能在一张卡上微调大模型The memory bill: why LoRA and QLoRA fit big models on one GPU

混合精度 AdamW 训练时，每个参数要存：BF16 权重 2 字节、梯度 2 字节、FP32 主权重 4 字节、Adam 的两个状态 8 字节，共 16 字节。LoRA 冻结的权重只需存 2 字节，梯度和优化器状态只为很少的 LoRA 参数保存。QLoRA 再把冻结的权重压缩到 4 位。激活值另算，它取决于批大小和序列长度。 With mixed-precision AdamW, each parameter needs a 2-byte BF16 weight, a 2-byte gradient, a 4-byte FP32 master copy and 8 bytes of Adam state: 16 bytes in total. LoRA keeps frozen weights at 2 bytes and stores gradients and optimizer state only for the few LoRA parameters. QLoRA also squeezes the frozen weights into 4 bits. Activations come on top and depend on batch size and sequence length.

6QLoRA 的 NF4：把 16 个等级放在最需要的地方QLoRA's NF4: put the 16 levels where they are needed

4 位只能表示 16 个值。量化时先把每一块（例如 64 个）权重除以块内的最大绝对值，缩放到 [−1, 1]，再取最近的等级。神经网络的权重大致呈正态分布，大多数值挤在 0 附近。INT4 的等级均匀分布；NF4 按正态分布的分位数放置等级，0 附近更密，所以误差更小。 4 bits can represent only 16 values. Each block of weights (say 64) is divided by its largest magnitude to fit in [−1, 1], then every weight snaps to the nearest level. Neural-network weights are roughly normal, so most values crowd near 0. INT4 spaces its levels evenly; NF4 places them at quantiles of a normal distribution, denser near 0, so its error is smaller.
