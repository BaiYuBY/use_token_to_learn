2RNN：梯度为什么会消失RNN: why the gradient vanishes

一个最简单的 RNN：ht = tanh(w·ht−1 + xt)。第一个输入对第 t 步的影响，等于每一步的导数 w·(1 − h²) 连乘。|w| 小于 1 时，乘积按指数缩小；大于 1 时可能爆炸。LSTM 的记忆用加法更新，影响约等于遗忘门 f 的连乘：f 接近 1，信息就能传很远。 The simplest RNN: ht = tanh(w·ht−1 + xt). The effect of the first input on step t is the product of every step's derivative w·(1 − h²). With |w| below 1 the product shrinks exponentially; above 1 it can explode. An LSTM updates its memory by addition, so the effect is roughly a product of forget gates f: with f near 1, information travels far.

3KNN：找最近的 k 个邻居投票KNN: let the k nearest neighbours vote

点击图中任意位置放一个查询点，连线的是它最近的 k 个邻居，按多数票决定类别。背景色是整张图的预测结果（决策边界）。k 太小，边界会把噪声也“记住”；k 太大，边界又太平滑。 Click anywhere to place a query point; lines connect it to its k nearest neighbours, and the majority decides its class. The background shows the prediction everywhere (the decision boundary). Too small a k memorizes the noise; too large a k smooths everything away.

维度灾难The curse of dimensionality

在 d 维单位立方体里随机撒 500 个点，看一个随机查询点到最近点和最远点的距离。维度越高，两者越接近，“最近邻”就越没有意义。 Scatter 500 random points in a d-dimensional unit cube and measure a random query's distance to the nearest and farthest point. The higher the dimension, the closer the two get, and the less “nearest” means.

4和 Transformer 的根本区别：串行还是并行The root difference from Transformers: sequential or parallel

按“播放”看训练时处理一段 12 个词元的序列：RNN 必须一个接一个地算（第 t 步要等第 t−1 步的隐藏状态）；Transformer 的注意力让每个位置直接看到前面所有位置，整段一次算完。生成时两者都只能逐个词元进行，但 RNN 只带一个固定大小的状态，Transformer 要保存不断增长的 KV 缓存。 Press play to process a 12-token sequence during training. The RNN must compute one step after another (step t waits for step t−1's hidden state); the Transformer's attention lets every position see all earlier ones directly, so the whole sequence is computed at once. When generating, both go one token at a time, but the RNN carries a fixed-size state while the Transformer keeps a growing KV cache.
