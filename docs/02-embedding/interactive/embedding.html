词元编号是怎样变成有“意义”的向量的？本页所有向量都是用 PyTorch 在小语料上真实训练出来的。 How does a token id become a vector with “meaning”? Every vector on this page was really trained with PyTorch on a small corpus.

1嵌入层就是一张查找表An embedding layer is a lookup table

嵌入矩阵 W 每一行对应一个词元。“查表”取出第 i 行，和“one-hot 向量乘以 W”的结果完全相同，但查表不需要做乘法。点一个词试试（这里只显示 8 个词，每个词的 16 维都画出来了）。 Each row of the embedding matrix W belongs to one token. Looking up row i gives exactly the same result as multiplying a one-hot vector by W, without doing any multiplication. Click a word (only 8 words are shown, each with all 16 dimensions).

2语义空间：拖动旋转，回放训练The semantic space: drag to rotate, replay training

16 维向量用 PCA 降到 3 维显示。拖动可以旋转，点一个点会连出它最相似的 5 个词。拖动“训练轮数”或点播放，看词向量如何从随机位置出发，按类别逐渐聚在一起。 The 16-d vectors are shown in 3-D using PCA. Drag to rotate; click a point to connect it to its 5 most similar words. Drag the epoch slider or press play to watch the vectors start from random positions and gather by category.

3怎样衡量“相似”：余弦相似度Measuring “similar”: cosine similarity

拖动两个箭头的端点。余弦相似度只看方向（夹角），不看长度：同向为 1，垂直为 0，反向为 −1。点积则同时受长度影响。 Drag the tips of the two arrows. Cosine similarity only looks at direction (the angle), not length: 1 when aligned, 0 when perpendicular, −1 when opposite. The dot product also depends on length.

cos(a, b) = a·b / (‖a‖ ‖b‖)
词向量的长度常常和词频有关，而我们关心的是“含义的方向”，所以比较词向量时通常用余弦相似度。 A word vector's length often tracks word frequency, while what we care about is the direction of its meaning, so word vectors are usually compared by cosine.

4向量算术：king − man + woman ≈ ?Vector arithmetic: king − man + woman ≈ ?

如果“男 → 女”在空间里是一个固定的方向，那么从 king 出发，减去 man、加上 woman，就应该落在 queen 附近。结果按与 A − B + C 的余弦相似度排序，并排除了 A、B、C 本身。 If “male → female” is a fixed direction in the space, then starting at king, subtracting man and adding woman should land near queen. Results are ranked by cosine similarity to A − B + C, excluding A, B and C themselves.
