RAG（检索增强生成）先从知识库里找出相关的文字，再连同问题一起交给模型。这一页的知识库是一批虚构人物的档案（模型训练时没见过这些人），检索结果和向量都来自本章代码训练的小型稠密编码器。 RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) first finds relevant text in a knowledge base, then hands it to the model together with the question. The knowledge base here is a set of profiles of made-up people (the encoder never saw them in training); retrieval results and vectors come from this chapter's small dense encoder.
